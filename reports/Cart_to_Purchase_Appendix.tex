\section*{Appendix: Supplementary Material}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix: Supplementary Material}

This appendix provides supplementary figures for the cart-to-purchase exploratory analysis on the Synerise RecSys~2025 active-user cohort ($K\geq 5$). The analysis is conducted at the unique user--SKU level: a pair converts if a purchase of that SKU occurs strictly after the first add-to-cart. The main text reports the corresponding conversion rates and time-gap statistics; the figures below visualize conversion composition, the lag distribution among converters, and coarse timing buckets.

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/cart_to_purchase_conversion.png}
\caption{Cart-to-purchase conversion on the active-user cohort. Left: 24.8\% of carted user--SKU pairs subsequently convert. Right: 81.7\% of purchased pairs had a prior same-SKU cart-add.}
\label{fig:app-conversion}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/cart_to_purchase_time_gap.png}
\caption{Cart-to-purchase lag for converted pairs. Left: lag histogram capped at P99 (median $\approx$11~minutes). Right: empirical CDF on a log-hour scale; 72\% / 87\% / 95\% of converters within 1~h / 24~h / 7~d.}
\label{fig:app-timegap}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/cart_to_purchase_gap_buckets.png}
\caption{Converted pairs by timing bucket: 72.1\% within 0--1h, 14.4\% within 1--24h, 9.0\% within 1--7d, 3.6\% within 7--30d, and 0.9\% after 30~days.}
\label{fig:app-buckets}
\end{figure}
