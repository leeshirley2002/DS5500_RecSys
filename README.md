# DS5500_RecSys
## Behavior-Aware Sequential Recommendation for Next Purchase Prediction

Online retailers often have limited visibility into what a customer wants to buy next because purchases occur relatively infrequently and reveal intent only after a transaction has already happened. Recommendation systems that rely mainly on purchase history can therefore overemphasize older preferences and miss products the customer is actively considering. Cart behavior may provide a more immediate signal of changing intent: adding an item can indicate interest, while removing it may suggest that interest has weakened or shifted. For retailers, the practical challenge is determining whether these signals are informative enough to improve recommendations and justify the additional engineering effort required to capture and model them.

This project investigates whether incorporating cart history leads to more accurate next-purchase recommendations than relying on purchase history alone. We use the Synerise RecSys 2025 dataset, which contains six months of real-world retail activity, including purchases, cart actions, visits, searches, and product information. We compare a recommendation approach based only on past purchases with behavior-aware versions that also incorporate cart activity, and measure whether the added information helps rank the product a user actually purchases next more highly. The final result shows whether cart behavior provides meaningful value beyond purchase history for next-purchase recommendation.
